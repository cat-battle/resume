1 What Happened & What Needs Replacing

A valve spring failed, which let one of the valves drop into the engine. When a valve drops, it can bounce around in the cylinder and cause serious damage to the cylinder head — including the valve seat, valve guides, and the head surface itself. The failed spring also bent a pushrod.

Even if the damage only affected one side, the entire top end is coming apart anyway, which makes this the right time to do a proper rebuild and delete the MDS system.

What is MDS and why delete it?
MDS (Multi-Displacement System) is Chrysler’s cylinder deactivation system — it shuts down 4 of the 8 cylinders at highway speeds to save fuel. The hardware that makes this work puts extra stress on valve springs, and failed valve springs are one of the most common HEMI failure modes. Deleting MDS eliminates this failure point entirely. It’s the fix, not just a repair.
What parts are typically replaced in a top-end rebuild?

Definitely replacing: both cylinder heads (or at minimum the damaged one), camshaft, all 16 lifters, all 16 pushrods, all valve springs, and all head gaskets.

Inspect and likely replace: rocker arms, rocker arm bolts, timing chain/tensioner (since everything is apart).

MDS delete also requires: a PCM tune to disable cylinder deactivation in software. Some kits include a tuner; others don’t. See the Important Notes section.

3 Cylinder Heads
Key question: Does he need one head or two? A machine shop can inspect both heads once they’re off. The side where the valve dropped is almost certainly damaged. The other side may still be fine — a shop can resurface it and reuse it. Budget for at least one new or reman head; two if both are compromised.

8 Important Notes
  • 🖥
    MDS Delete Requires a PCM Tune
    When deleting MDS, the truck’s computer (PCM/ECM) needs to be reprogrammed to disable cylinder deactivation in software — otherwise the computer will still try to activate it and cause issues. Some kits include a Diablo Predator 2 or similar handheld tuner. If the kit doesn’t include one, budget an extra $350–500 for a tuner.
  • 🚼
    Oil After MDS Delete
    With MDS removed, the engine no longer needs Mopar’s MS-6395 oil additive specification. Any quality 5W-20 full synthetic will work great — Castrol Edge is a solid choice.
  • 🌳
    MDS Delete is Legal in Oregon
    MDS delete is not legal in California (emissions), but Oregon is fine. No issues there.
  • 📦
    Core Charges on Reman Engines
    Some remanufactured engines have a core charge ($500–770) that is refunded when you return the old engine. Factor this into the upfront cost — you’ll get it back.
