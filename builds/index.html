Important Considerations
  • 8GB VRAM caveat: 8GB is adequate for 1440p60 today but may become limiting in 2–3 years for newer titles at high settings.
  • RX 7600 real-world: Handles 1440p medium-high at 60fps in most current titles — Cyberpunk, Hogwarts Legacy, Baldur's Gate 3.
  • Esports titles: For Valorant, CS2, Fortnite — expect 1440p 100+ fps with ease.
  • Monitor: A solid 1440p 60Hz monitor can be found for $150–200 if needed.
  • Timing: Black Friday / Cyber Monday is coming up — consider waiting for deals on both prebuilts and parts.
