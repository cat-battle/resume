Prebuilt vs. Custom — the uncomfortable truth: With the STGAubron prebuilt at $740 including an i7 + RX 7600, the cheapest custom build (Build 3 at ~$869) costs more and requires assembly time. The prebuilt is genuinely competitive here.

However: prebuilt PSUs and motherboards are often cheap or proprietary. A custom build gives better component quality and much easier future upgrades.

📝
Important Considerations
  • 8GB VRAM caveat: 8GB is adequate for 1440p60 today but may become limiting in 2–3 years for newer titles at high settings.
  • RX 7600 real-world: Handles 1440p medium-high at 60fps in most current titles — Cyberpunk, Hogwarts Legacy, Baldur’s Gate 3. Esports titles (Valorant, CS2) hit 100+ fps easily.
  • ASRock B550M-HDV note: Only 2 RAM slots on this board. Use a 2×8GB kit, not a single 16GB stick.
  • DDR5 sticker shock: $250 for 16GB DDR5 vs. $100 for DDR4 is a ~$150 difference. This is the main cost driver for AM5 builds right now.
  • Monitor: A solid 1440p 60Hz monitor can be found for $150–200. Budget for it if needed.
